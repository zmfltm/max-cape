Which Path

267,273,120 XP left. What that costs in hours is entirely a question of how much attention you are willing to pay.

Numbered in the order in which they are worth doing: skills that hand XP to other skills come first, so the gift lands before you pay for those levels. The calculation then follows that order, so each skill shows the level you will actually reach rather than the one you are on today. 23 of 48 diary tiers are already covered by your current levels; the rest are marked on the step that brings them in range. Diary tiers also require quest points and items, which this calculation does not account for, and the XP lamps they award are not counted.
